Instrument sounds rendered from the Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont
(FluidR3_GM.sf2, Debian package fluid-soundfont-gm 3.1) by tools/build_gm.mjs:
one note every 3 semitones C2-C6 per instrument, rendered dry by FluidSynth,
trimmed to 3.3 s, 320 kbps MP3.
